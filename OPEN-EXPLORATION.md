# Open exploration and boss passages

Reachable battles, shops, treasures and healing locations are usable immediately. Normal locations no longer require an earlier story stage. Battles may be approached in any order within the current region.

Each region's boss lies beyond a visible sealed passage. Clearing the three other main battles and the region's side battle opens that passage. Random wandering battles are not required. Supply caches, springs and shrine choices remain optional. Previously defeated bosses keep their passages open in older saves.

Closed passages physically block the trail and list remaining battles when approached. Old saved positions beyond an unopened passage return to the closest safe trail before it.
