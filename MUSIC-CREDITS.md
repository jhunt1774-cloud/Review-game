# Orchestral soundtrack credits

## Commercial use requirement

The game may be sold in the future. Music added to this project must permit commercial use and redistribution inside a paid game. Do not include music limited to personal, educational, or noncommercial use.

Both included tracks are published under CC0 on their authors’ source pages. [Creative Commons’ CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) expressly permit copying, modification, distribution, and performance for commercial purposes without asking permission. On those published terms, both tracks may be included in a paid game without royalties. Artist credits and source links are retained here for provenance.

- Composer: **Theodore Kerr**, OpenGameArt username **Wolfgang_**
- Source: [Battle Theme on OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/node/16611)
- License shown by the author: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

These are full orchestral compositions by the credited artists, not music composed by Codex. The artists’ original compressed recordings are embedded in `music-data.js` and in the standalone game. The current music player does not call external hosts at runtime. Travel uses the same continuous soundtrack across regions; battle switches to its own soundtrack. Volume changes and transition fades happen during playback.

The initial proposal considered Emma_MA’s Determined Pursuit; the shipped battle track is Theodore Kerr’s Battle Theme. Determined Pursuit is not included in this package.
