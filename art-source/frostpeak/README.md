# Frostpeak painted layout and Blender terrain study

This design replaces the short Frostpeak trail blockout as the map reference. The lower meadow loops past a ruined arch to the journal. A side climb reaches the station and watchtower, while the timber crossing leads to the mine's graded switchbacks and an additional lookout. The art uses the painted Frostpeak and River Valley maps as references for style and scale.

The Blender study pins the painting to a fixed orthographic camera and projects 20 landmarks and eight connected route groups onto elevation-aware geometry. The design spans 132 Blender meters in the camera. The generator checks connectivity and caps the grade of each authored segment at 35%. The current scene contains route surfaces and the reference art. Terrain between routes, collision, foreground scenery and the browser export still need to be built.

## Image prompt

Paint an original expansive Frostpeak Pass environment design for Forest Path: Web Wizardry RPG using the two attached game map images only as aesthetic and scale references. Reference 1 supplies the very specific hand-painted storybook alpine material and crisp game-world readability: warm stone trail, slate cliffs with patches of blue snow, olive groundcover and dimensional rock edges. Reference 2 supplies the larger continuous walkable environment scale, little landmarks separated by many character lengths, textured ground, three-quarter game-camera projection, and deliberate path junctions. Create a distinctly NEW mountainous landscape with deep terrain, far more explorable than one winding corridor. Fixed orthographic three-quarter bird's-eye game view about 48 degrees down from horizontal, horizontal 3:2 composition, no horizon. The players will pan and zoom into this whole map, so make large usable walking areas occupy most of the frame and keep buildings small at character scale. Design a broad lower valley meadow with dusting of snow and clustered alpine firs in bottom left, visible branching trails that meet again, a snowmelt creek sunk below the main valley with a narrow timber crossing at lower center and deep, visibly vertical rock sides, broken supply cart and journal at a sheltered bend on the lower route, a warm timber and masonry climbing station with lamps and supply stacks on a sizable middle terrace to left of center, a broad visibly graded switchback up the east side connecting elevations, a separate high western watchtower overlook with a return loop, and a distinct dark timber-supported mine portal cut into a high slate wall on the upper right with a usable flat approach. Layer four height levels using exposed stratified retaining cliffs, sculpted ridges, grade changes, shadows under ledges and creek erosion. Wide paths without blind turns, patchy ochre trail, snow melt edge and scattered ground grasses. Natural terrain continues to edges. Detailed, hand-painted environment textures and lovingly finished props like the game's reference, expressive warm sunlight on snow and cool shadows, not photoreal and no low-poly primitives. Keep path connectivity visually unambiguous, no overlapping roads at different heights unless a clearly visible bridge connects them. No UI, labels, grids, people, arrows, border, lettering, panels or printed map texture. Full polished environment concept for building a real 2.5D Blender terrain and separable foreground assets.
