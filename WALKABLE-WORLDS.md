# Walkable worlds update

Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the four touch buttons. The party walks directly on each region map. Road boundaries block water and impassable terrain. Nearby locations become visible as you approach. Reaching supplies, springs, camps, shrines, or enemies triggers the event automatically. The old destination-click travel cutscene is bypassed during exploration.

Long walks can trigger wandering enemies. Win and return to exploration at the saved position. Rewards for wandering encounters are limited to one collection per region. Main encounters still unlock the next story stages. Completed encounters remain cleared. This is path-based exploration rather than unrestricted movement across every painted surface.

All four chapters, including Sunken Coast, are included. Existing saves, teacher assignments, high-contrast questions and hero action menus are preserved.

Upload all extracted contents to the repository root, replacing matching files. Confirm exploration.js and coast.js appear beside index.html.
