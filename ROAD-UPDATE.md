# Walking journeys and road encounters

The map remains the route chooser. Travel switches into a ground-level chapter scene where all four heroes walk to the destination. On longer trips (25+ road units), after at least two journeys, there is a 35% chance of an ambush. A road encounter uses the current question set and defense system. Win and choose Continue to resume the original destination. Defeat offers retry; save/continue preserves the interrupted journey.

Road rewards and forge runes are awarded once per region to prevent farming. Travel ambushes reuse existing animated enemies. Camp and shrine travel is safe; regular battle, spring and supply destinations can trigger ambushes. This is automatic travel, not free movement controlled with arrow keys.
