# Forest reward equipment update

Forest victories now offer optional equipment choices directly on the earned item card. Weapons offer the matching hero; Bridgeguard armor offers all four companions. Each choice shows the actual change in stats and confirms the wearer. Continue remains available without equipping; camp retains its existing equipment management.

Only the current, owned, completed encounter reward can be equipped during victory. Repeated clicks, invalid roles, earlier rewards and other game phases are rejected. Equipment uses the existing inventory and save format; no automatic healing is added. The full campaign keeps its camp equipment rules.

Validation: static/import checks, Pages package build, new browser reward checks, existing combat payoff checks and complete forest adventure browser test.

Production remains unchanged until the feature branch is merged. Physical Chromebook performance and a timed classroom playthrough remain unverified.
