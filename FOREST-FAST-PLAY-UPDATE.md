# Forest fast-play and interface update

The default brief-story preference is now presented as Fast play. The forest quest starts with one short exposition. Encounter arrivals immediately enter combat after assets prepare; players no longer stop at a narrative/briefing screen before every fight. Victories use a short result and Keep going action, while reward equipment and optional rune spending remain available. Correct-answer feedback advances after two seconds; incorrect answers keep five seconds, and opening Why pauses the review. The final story is one line, with run statistics inside an optional summary. Full story remains an existing Settings option.

A compact clickable destination map sits at the lower left, above the controls. It shows Gate, Hollow, Bridge, Heart and Camp, plus optional supply/spirit/ranger buttons once unlocked. Completed/next/locked states and the party position are visible. Destinations use the existing collision-aware pathfinder. The long gold and blue travel lines, duplicate route panel and extra direction marker are removed in Fast play; walking, keyboard takeover, Stop/Escape and a small destination marker remain.

The permanent map toolbar is reduced to Party and contextual interaction/Stop controls. Skills, equipment, forge, quests, world changes, atlas and camp-travel actions remain accessible from Party. Appearance and traits are collapsed during hero creation. NPC descriptions and choice labels are shorter, with costs/benefits retained. Combat abilities, questions, defense timing, potions, equipment, upgrades and meaningful preparation choices are unchanged.

The required imp and wolf now patrol their own clearings, alert and pursue using the existing roaming/collision logic. Contact starts their existing m0/m1 encounters, so no extra required fights or patrol save IDs were introduced. Static duplicate enemies and invisible fixed-point auto-starts are suppressed for these two encounters. The sentinel stays at the bridge and the Guardian at the tree. Flee grace remains in effect, and completed encounters do not respawn. Patrols wait for their sprite to load before engaging. Restoring the Heart clears hostile patrols.

Validation: static/import/syntax checks and Pages package; the full forest regression suite; a new default-mode browser test covering the opening, non-overlapping map labels, destination clicks, moving/collision-safe patrols, real encounter contact, direct combat, save validation, reward equip, concise spirit choice, Party features, full-story fallback, Guardian focus/shield bonuses, short ending and 768 x 600 layout. Existing detailed-story tests explicitly select Full story to retain coverage of that optional flow. All ten map layouts, 114 routes, twelve sealed destinations and five entrance/exit round trips are checked, alongside the original campaign/import/save smoke test.

Physical Chromebook performance and timed student engagement still need a classroom playtest. No backend, account system, save-format migration or new image assets were added.

Test browsers now cap their disposable disk caches and remove their profiles on exit. Old scratch screenshots/profiles and the rebuildable local site package were cleared after the local drive filled; final functional browser checks passed afterward.
